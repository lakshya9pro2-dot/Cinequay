## 🗺️ How to Use the Website

Here's a simple, step-by-step guide to get started on Cinequay.

### Step 1: Open the Website

Go to the Cinequay website in your browser. You'll land on the **homepage**, which gives you an overview of the platform.

At the top of the page, you'll see navigation buttons including **Login** and **Register**. If you're new, you'll need to create a free account first.

---

### Step 2: Create Your Account

Click the **Register** button.

Fill in:
- Your **email address**
- A **password** (at least 6 characters)
- (Optional) A **referral code**, if someone invited you — this gives them a small bonus

Click **Register**, and your account is created instantly. You'll be taken directly to your **Dashboard**.

> 💡 **Tip:** If you have a referral code from a friend, make sure to enter it during registration — you and your friend both benefit!

---

### Step 3: Explore Your Dashboard

Once you're logged in, you'll see your **Dashboard** — this is your personal control center.

From here, you can:
- See an **overview** of your videos, views, and earnings
- Go to **My Videos** to see all the videos you've uploaded
- Use the **Upload** tab to add new videos
- Check your **Earnings** and request payouts
- Manage your **API Keys** if you're a developer
- Update your **Settings** (payout method, account details)

The dashboard is divided into clear sections — just click the tab that matches what you want to do.

---

### Step 4: Upload Your First Video

Click on the **Upload** section in your dashboard (or visit the Upload page directly).

You have two options:

**Option A — Direct Upload:**
- Drag and drop your video file into the upload area, or click to browse your files
- Enter a **title** for your video
- (Optional) Add a description and choose who can see it (Public, Unlisted, or Private)
- Click **Upload**

**Option B — Remote Upload:**
- Go to the **Remote Upload** section
- Paste the **URL** of a video you want to import
- Enter a title
- Click **Submit**

After uploading, Cinequay will begin processing your video. You'll see a progress indicator showing the status (Processing → Ready). Once it's ready, your video is live!

---

### Step 5: Share Your Video

Once your video is ready, go to **My Videos** and find your video. Click on it to see:

- A **shareable link** you can send to anyone: `https://cinequay.site/v/your-video-id`
- An **embed code** to paste into your website or blog

Share your link anywhere — social media, forums, messaging apps, or your own website.

---

### Step 6: Watch Your Earnings Grow

Every time someone watches your video for long enough, it counts as a **qualified view** and adds a small amount to your balance.

Go to the **Earnings** tab in your dashboard to see:
- How much you've earned **today**
- How much you've earned **this month**
- Your **total lifetime earnings**
- Your **current balance**

### Step 7: Request a Payout

When your balance reaches the minimum payout amount, you can request a withdrawal.

1. Go to the **Earnings** tab
2. Click **Request Payout**
3. Enter the amount you want to withdraw

An admin will review and process your payout within 24 hours.

## 📤 How to Upload

Uploading a video on Cinequay is easy. Here's exactly what to do:

### Finding the Upload Option

- Log in to your account
- Click the **Upload** link in the top menu, or go to the Upload tab in your Dashboard

### What to Do

1. Click the upload area or drag your video file into it
2. Your video will start uploading immediately — you'll see a progress bar
3. While uploading, fill in:
   - **Title** (required)
   - **Description** (optional)
   - **Visibility**: Public (anyone can see it), Unlisted (only people with the link can see it), or Private (only you)
4. After the file uploads, Cinequay processes it automatically — this may take a few minutes
5. Once the status changes to **Ready**, your video is live and shareable

### Supported File Types

Cinequay accepts common video formats including **MP4, MKV, AVI, WebM, MOV**, and more. Most video files you record on a phone, camera, or computer will work.

### What Happens After Uploading

After you upload, Cinequay:
- Stores your video securely
- Replicates it across multiple backup servers for reliability
- Generates a shareable link and embed code
- Shows you the video status in your dashboard

### What to Do If the Upload Fails

- Check that your internet connection is stable
- Make sure your file is a video format (not a photo or document)
- Try refreshing the page and uploading again
- If the problem continues, try using the **Remote Upload** option instead (paste a URL to your video)

## 💡 Tips for Getting the Best Results

Here are some practical tips to help you get the most out of Cinequay:

**1. Use a good title.** A clear, descriptive title helps people find and understand your video. Avoid vague titles like "Video 1."

**2. Share your video widely.** The more people who see your video, the more qualified views you earn. Post your link on social media, forums, and communities where your audience hangs out.

**3. Check the video status before sharing.** Wait until the status shows **Ready** before sharing your link. If you share before it's ready, viewers may not be able to watch it yet.

**4. Set the right privacy.** If you want everyone to see it, choose **Public**. If you want only specific people to access it, choose **Unlisted** and only share the link with those people.

**5. Keep your videos focused.** Viewers are more likely to watch a full video if it's focused and engaging. Longer, quality watch times mean more qualified views and more earnings.

**6. Check your earnings regularly.** Log in to your Dashboard often to track your performance and see which videos are earning the most.

**7. Update your payout settings early.** Before requesting a payout, make sure your payment details are saved correctly in **Settings** so there are no delays.

**8. Use Remote Upload for large files.** If you have a very large video file that's slow to upload from your device, try using the Remote Upload option with a direct download link instead.
